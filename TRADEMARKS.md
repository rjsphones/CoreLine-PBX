# CoreLine Names and Branding

CoreLine, CoreLine PBX, CoreLine Connect, associated logos, product appearance, and related names identify the CoreLine project.

You may refer to CoreLine truthfully, link to the official project, and state that a device or service is compatible when that statement is accurate.

You may not use CoreLine branding to imply that an unofficial build, fork, download, service, repository, community, or commercial product is official, approved, sponsored, or operated by CoreLine.

Contact CoreLine before using its logo or branding in commercial material.
