# CoreLine Roadmap

The roadmap describes direction, not a promise of delivery dates or finished functionality.

## Current priorities

1. Reliable local and remote softphone calling
2. CoreLine Connect authentication, call routing, NAT, and media reliability
3. Safe upgrades and automatic rollback
4. Backup verification and restore testing
5. Call history and voicemail improvements
6. Security hardening and permission controls
7. Phone provisioning and Cisco XML reliability

## Trunking research

CoreLine Trunking is an idea under evaluation. Public telephone service requires carrier agreements, fraud controls, billing, taxes, number administration, emergency calling, registered locations, acceptable-use rules, support procedures, and legal review before launch.

## How to suggest changes

Use the GitHub feature-request form. Explain the user problem, desired behavior, affected devices, and why the feature belongs in CoreLine.
