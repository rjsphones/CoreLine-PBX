# Community Code of Conduct

CoreLine welcomes respectful users, testers, phone enthusiasts, administrators, and developers.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Discuss technical disagreements without attacking people.
- Protect other users' privacy and credentials.
- Report security problems privately.
- Give accurate credit.
- Follow community, carrier, telecommunications, and emergency-service rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexual content
- Publishing private information or credentials
- Encouraging toll fraud, unauthorized access, spoofing abuse, or illegal calling
- Impersonating CoreLine staff or claiming unofficial builds are official
- Spam, repeated demands, or intentionally misleading reports

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and community. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method at https://corelines.xyz.
